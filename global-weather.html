Weather forecasts for cities worldwide

Use the city search to explore weather conditions in destinations across different countries. Results may include current temperature, feels-like temperature, relative humidity, wind speed and a 7-day outlook.

How to use Global Weather

  1. Enter a city name, such as Tokyo, London, New York or Manila.
  2. Select Search to find the location and load its weather.
  3. Review current conditions and the 7-day forecast.
  4. Alternatively, select Use My Location and grant location permission if you want local weather.

Weather conditions can change quickly. Check official local weather services for emergency alerts and critical safety decisions.
